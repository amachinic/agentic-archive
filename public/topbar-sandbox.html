Top bar sandbox

Four ways to merge the prompt component and the keyterm filter into one landscape strip at the very top of the field, cosmos-style. Everything below each bar is a stand-in for the canvas. All four are wired enough to feel: open the categories, scroll the river, type in the command bar, switch the tabs.
